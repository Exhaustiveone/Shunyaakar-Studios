        The person behind the zero

        I'm Mayank Sharma, a filmmaker, photographer and creative director from Jaipur. Shunyaakar started as the name on my photographs. Now it's the house where my films and music get made, and this site is its open notebook.

        I write, direct, shoot and cut my own films, mostly on Sundays, mostly with no lighting kit and a lot of stubbornness. Everything I learn along the way goes up here, from the first draft to the final grade.
